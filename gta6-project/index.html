Welcome to Leonida

Leonida is the fictional state built as GTA's take on Florida — swampland, strip malls, small towns, and one unmistakable skyline rising out of the coast. Vice City anchors it all: the same neon-soaked energy the series is known for, rebuilt from the ground up for a new generation.

It's described as the largest and most detailed world Rockstar has built for the series — more coastline, more interiors, more reasons to slow down and actually look at the place instead of just driving through it.

This is a fan concept, so consider this a mood board rather than a map — built around what's been publicly confirmed, not a leak or a preview of unreleased content.

Two people, one story

The reveal trailer introduced a duo whose fates are tangled together from the first scene — running toward something and away from everything else at the same time.

L

Lucia Caminos

Confirmed as one of the two lead characters — and the first playable woman to headline a Grand Theft Auto story. Sharp, guarded, and done waiting for things to get better on their own.

J

Jason Duval

The other half of the story. Where their history began and where it's headed is still mostly a mystery — which is exactly why fans keep replaying every frame of both trailers.

How we got here

A quick history of everything officially confirmed since the first reveal.

December 2023

The reveal trailer

Rockstar's first trailer confirmed the return of Vice City, introduced Lucia and Jason, and broke online video-viewing records within its first day.

May 2025

Trailer two

A second trailer expanded the world — more of Leonida, more vehicles, and a story that leans further into social-media-era chaos than any prior entry.

May 2025

First delay

Days after the second trailer, Rockstar pushed the release from fall 2025 to May 26, 2026, citing the need for more polish.

2026

Second delay

The release window shifted again, this time to November 19, 2026 — Take-Two has since reaffirmed the date is on track.

November 19, 2026

Launch day

The current, officially confirmed release date for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S.
